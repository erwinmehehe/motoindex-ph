# MotoIndex PH v1.1 — High-volume helmet brand depth

## What shipped

The five highest-priority helmet brands from the PH keyword map now have source-backed brand guides and product records:

- KYT — 3 checked models
- Spyder — 3 checked models
- Gille — 3 checked models
- EVO — 3 checked models
- SEC — 3 checked models

Each checked product can store and display helmet type, observed price, size list when available, certification notes, shell notes, visor configuration, source URL, separate price-source URL and last-checked date.

## Search-quality safeguards

- Research-only helmet product pages are `noindex`.
- Research-only helmet products are excluded from the sitemap.
- Helmet brand hubs enter the sitemap only after they have at least two verified/source-backed products.
- Low-priority brands with insufficient data can remain accessible for internal research without becoming indexable SEO pages.
- Price copy is described as an observed/source price rather than a guaranteed current market price.
- Public pages remind riders to check the PS/ICC mark on the exact helmet sold in the Philippines.
