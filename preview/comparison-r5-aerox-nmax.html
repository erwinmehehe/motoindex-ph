Detailed comparison

Aerox vs NMAX price

Aerox V3 is currently ₱125,900–₱163,900; NMAX V3 is ₱155,900–₱175,900. These are dated observed records, not a promise of dealer inventory.

Engine, horsepower and torque

Both list 155 cc. Aerox lists 15.4 hp and 14.2 Nm; NMAX lists 15.1 hp and 14.2 Nm. Output is compared on paper only.

Seat height and rider fit

NMAX has a 20 mm lower published seat. Seat width, suspension sag, footwear and rider proportions still affect actual ground reach.

Fuel tank and fuel economy

NMAX carries 7.1 L versus Aerox at 5.5 L. Comparable published km/L figures are not currently stored for both records.

Storage and everyday practicality

MotoIndex does not yet list directly comparable storage-capacity figures for both motorcycles, so the page does not invent a liters figure.

Which is easier for city use?

Aerox is lighter and cheaper on the current records; NMAX has the lower seat. Those facts can inform the choice without claiming one is universally easier in traffic.
