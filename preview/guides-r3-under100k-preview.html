Methodology

How we selected these motorcycles

The list is ordered by observed starting price from lowest to highest. If two models have similar prices, lower curb weight is used as a secondary comparison factor.

Inclusion rule
  • Current MotoIndex motorcycle record.
  • Observed entry price below ₱100,000.
  • A model may qualify even when a higher trim exceeds ₱100K.
Tie-breaker
  • Lower curb weight.
  • Do not interpret price order as an overall quality ranking.
Data source policy

Observed prices and specifications stay separate. Each model links to its source-backed page and verification date.

Important caveats

  • A model can qualify when its entry variant is below ₱100,000 even if higher trims exceed the budget.
  • Observed dealer/market prices can differ from manufacturer SRP and change with stock, location and promotions.
  • Published fuel-economy figures can use different test conditions and are not perfectly comparable real-world results.
  • Seat height alone does not determine physical rider fit.

Questions about this guide

FAQ content follows actual search intent and links users into relevant model/guide pages.

What is the cheapest motorcycle in the Philippines under ₱100,000?

Among the current qualifying MotoIndex records in this preview, Honda Navi has the lowest observed starting price. Check the model page for its latest price-source date.

What automatic motorcycles cost below ₱100K?

The guide filters the current database and shows qualifying automatic motorcycles such as Honda BeAT, Yamaha Mio Gear, Suzuki Avenis and others when their observed entry prices remain below the threshold.

Are there motorcycles with ABS below ₱100K?

ABS availability is checked at variant level where the source supports it. This guide does not infer ABS from a model family name.

Does the ₱100K limit include all variants?

No. A model can qualify because its entry variant is below ₱100K even when a higher trim exceeds the budget.
