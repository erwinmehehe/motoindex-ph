Should you buy the CFMOTO 450MT in the Philippines?

A high-value middleweight adventure twin with serious wheel, tank and clearance numbers for riders who want one bike for city, provincial roads and longer trips.

Research angle

Middleweight PH adventure benchmark: price, real dealer support, expressway use, 21/18 tire supply and luggage ecosystem are the decision points.

Buy it if
  • You want a 449cc parallel twin with 21/18-inch adventure wheels.
  • A 17.5 L tank and 220 mm ground clearance fit your mixed-road plans.
  • You want more hardware than a 250cc budget adventure bike without premium-brand pricing.
Skip it if
  • A low seat and very light parking weight are priorities.
  • You want a huge used-market history before buying.
  • You mainly ride short city trips where a lighter scooter or roadster would be easier.
PH ownership reality
  • Dealer locations along your route and 21/18 tire availability matter as much as the spec sheet.
  • Confirm current toll-road and registration requirements for the exact unit.
  • Check luggage, rack and crash-protection availability before budgeting an adventure build.
