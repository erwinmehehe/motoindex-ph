# MotoIndex PH v1.9 — commute source ledger

Checked: 2026-08-25

## Why commute/affordability is a first-class product area

- Philippine Senate filing (2025), citing the 2024 TomTom Traffic Index: Davao City, Manila and Caloocan were among the cities with the highest rush-hour time loss / congestion measures in the cited dataset. This is used only as background context; MotoIndex does not estimate route-time savings.
  - https://legacy.senate.gov.ph/lisdata/4774243764%21.pdf
- LTFRB / Philippine News Agency (2026): motorcycle-taxi rider accreditation and provisional authority processing remain an active transport-policy area.
  - https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1280710

## Fuel-price reference

- Department of Energy — Energy Prices & Fuel Rate Monitors. The commuter calculator keeps gasoline price user-editable and links users to DOE rather than claiming the default is the current pump price everywhere.
  - https://new.doe.gov.ph/prices

## Registration / daily-use compliance

- LTO (2025): enforcement against unregistered vehicles; motorcycles made up the majority of vehicle apprehensions cited for June 2025. The rainy-season page links to this as a daily-use compliance reminder, not as a claim about crash risk.
  - https://lto.gov.ph/news/lto-warns-of-impounding-unregistered-motor-vehicles-as-over-20000-with-expired-registration-apprehended-in-june-2025/

## Safety boundaries used in v1.9

- No universal flood/wading-depth value is inferred from motorcycle ground clearance.
- "Heavy traffic" ranking is a planning score from curb weight, transmission, fuel economy, seat height and price—not a safety or travel-time score.
- Delivery/work ranking does not claim eligibility for any motorcycle-taxi, delivery or gig platform.
- Passenger ranking is a shortlist aid and does not claim measured pillion comfort.
- Financing/affordability uses user-selected inputs and standard amortization math; it is not financial advice or a lender quote.
