# MotoIndex product UX audit — 2026-09-15

This batch turns the existing research-heavy pages into a calmer buyer decision experience without changing canonical URLs, structured data, pricing logic, or source provenance.

## Design priorities

- Keep model heroes compact enough to show price, actions, image and key facts without a full-screen billboard.
- Use white media stages for white-background motorcycle photography; no dark spec overlays on the image.
- Keep section headings in a consistent product hierarchy rather than editorial display sizes.
- Present buyer verdicts, financing controls, colors, recommendations and recently viewed items as compact decision surfaces.
- Keep data tables readable and scannable with restrained borders, shadows and indigo accents.
- Preserve mobile overflow safety, reduced motion and Cloudflare/static rendering behavior.

## Product/SEO priorities

- Model pages remain the canonical home for price, specs, colors, fit, fuel, financing, maintenance, used value and alternatives.
- Buyer guidance and comparison links should help users make a decision rather than create thin SEO URLs.
- Source/freshness signals stay visible near purchase decisions.
- Recommendation hubs should route users into models, Finder, Compare and topic guides rather than become giant walls of cards.
